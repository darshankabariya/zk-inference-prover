**The verdict, in one paragraph.** The denominator is one number now: 300.97 M
cells/token at 27B/16K, so 53 tok/s needs 15.95 G cells/s. Re-reading B7b's
synthetic proxy against it moves nothing (×3.6 / ×5.5 / ×10.9 = 4.85 tok/s), but
the **real 8B chunk is 12–23% slower per cell than the proxy was**, and it is the
row that counts: commit path **×4.2**, stwo total **×7.2**, whole wall **×12.5** =
**4.24 tok/s, ×12.5 off 53**. The kinds of verdict hold — the commit path is
still ≤5× (confirmed) and the whole wall still inside 5–100× (an engineering gap,
same two hotspots) — with one exception: **stwo total is no longer at the 5×
line**. The two things that stopped a real
layer proving are both **witness-side and both preconditions of the same kind**:
the exp table's master exponent has to be layer-wide rather than band-wide, and
the attention-output fold's output scale has to be cut for the *rectangle the
AIR proves* rather than for the forward pass's masked output. Neither is a limit
of the design; neither moved a column, a constraint or a kernel. With both,
**all 24 Qwen-0.5B layers accept** and **a real Llama-3.1-8B layer at S=512,
C=16K proves at a 2.415 G chunk** with every identity true and its digest
matched by the SIMD reference. What the fixes cost is table rows: the shared
master table is **5.010 fresh cells per row** and its rows are `2^(d_bits +
spread)`, so it is 4% of a spread-0 layer proof and **59% of a spread-5 one** —
and pinning a whole model to one shape costs **×1.97** on Qwen-0.5B's 24 layers,
which is not "a few table rows". On the comparator, W8A16 is within noise of
fp32 everywhere, so the honest claim is "matches the strong INT8 baseline",
not "beats the strawman".

## 2. What B9 changes for the design

1. **The master exponent is a model-level constant, not a band-level one.**
   `Attn::build` takes it; `Layer::load` reads the layer-wide maximum out of the
   manifest. The price is `2^(d_bits + spread)` rows at **5.010 fresh cells
   each** — measured, against the plan's guess of ~7. At spread 5 that is 59% of
   the layer proof. **Spread, not depth, is what makes a layer expensive.**
2. **A sub-computation proof needs scales cut for the sub-computation.** The
   fold's `s_o` came from a forward pass that attended to the causal corner; the
   AIR proves only the rectangle. Its completeness precondition is
   `max |A·V·s_v/Z| ≤ 127·s_o` over the rows proved, and `extract.py` now
   asserts it. This is the same kind of precondition as the exp table's and
   belongs beside it in any statement of what the scheme requires.
3. **The 9-bit numerator is a bound change, not a layout change** — 0 cells per
   accumulator, no column moves — but it leaves B4's offset limb window (2²⁹)
   for M31's sign-bit window (2³⁰), which only the explicit-sign readings reach.
   10 bits is out of the field at C=16K.
4. **`Round::Away` on all seven layer matmuls, the power-of-two dequant on
   `lm_head` alone.** B6.5's mixed layout is retired; the planning range with it.
5. **One shape per model is a real trade, not a free one.** `--ao-rem-bits`
   makes it possible; ×1.97 on Qwen-0.5B makes it wrong there. Per-layer shapes
   (one more emit chain) win wherever the spread is not uniform.
6. **Static W8A8 is not the INT8 baseline.** W8A16 is, and it is free.

## 5. Rules that cost a session when forgotten

1. `off_table` is **one counter over every table** a composed layer builds. It
   names no table, and the two candidate fixes are opposite. Attribute it
   (`b9_diag.py`) before believing a diagnosis — the brief's was wrong, and the
   wrong fix was an AIR change with a kernel regeneration behind it.
2. The **CUDA tree fills the grid on the device**, so host-side counters in
   `attn_trace.rs` see nothing there. Diagnostics of the witness go through
   `b65_ref`, which is also where rung 1's digest comes from.
3. A witness that proves a **sub-computation** must be given that
   sub-computation's scales. Anything the forward pass calibrated on more rows
   than the AIR proves is a completeness bug waiting for the data to find it —
   1 row in 33 M is enough to reject an honest proof.
4. On the B8 path the forward pass is on the GPU, so every recorded column is a
   cuda tensor and `.numpy()` fails. `LastChunk.add` copies to the host.
5. B7A §5 rules 1, 2, 6, 9, 13 and B8 §5 1–8 carry unchanged. Every throughput
   number names its chunk size and all three boundaries; a new shape runs the
   emit chain first.
6. `b9_run.sh`'s steps are separate for a reason: `emit8b` rebuilds the
   generator tree (~3 min), `install8b` rebuilds `libstwo_cuda` (~70 s), and
   `shapes8b` rebuilds the proving tree. Only `prove8b` needs the GPU free.
